Why informed consent first

  • 40% of U.S. malpractice cases in CRICO/Candello's 2014–2024 analysis involved a communication-related factor; 63% of those, a provider–patient communication failure.
  • In surgical claims, inadequate informed consent — especially not explaining non-surgical alternatives — raises the chance of an indemnity payment.
  • If a shared-understanding interface works at the highest-stakes decision in medicine, it can extend to law, insurance, immigration, housing and finance: anywhere people must truly understand before they say “yes”.
